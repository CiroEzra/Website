Book Collection

Educated

By Tara Westover

Published: 2018

★★★★★ (4.7/5)

Memoir Autobiography Nonfiction

Tara Westover was 17 the first time she set foot in a classroom. Born to survivalists in the mountains of Idaho, she prepared for the end of the world by stockpiling home-canned peaches and sleeping with her "head-for-the-hills" bag. Her father forbade hospitals, so Tara never saw a doctor or nurse.

The Silent Patient

By Alex Michaelides

Published: 2019

★★★★☆ (4.2/5)

Psychological Thriller Mystery Fiction

Alicia Berenson's life is seemingly perfect. A famous painter married to an in-demand fashion photographer, she lives in a grand house overlooking a park in one of London's most desirable areas. One evening, her husband Gabriel returns home late, and Alicia shoots him five times in the face and then never speaks another word.

Atomic Habits

By James Clear

Published: 2018

★★★★★ (4.6/5)

Self-help Nonfiction Psychology

No matter your goals, Atomic Habits offers a proven framework for improving--every day. James Clear, one of the world's leading experts on habit formation, reveals practical strategies that will teach you exactly how to form good habits, break bad ones, and master the tiny behaviors that lead to remarkable results.

It Ends with Us

By Colleen Hoover

Published: 2016

★★★★☆ (4.4/5)

Romance Contemporary Fiction

Lily hasn't always had it easy, but that's never stopped her from working hard for the life she wants. When she feels a spark with a gorgeous neurosurgeon named Ryle Kincaid, everything in Lily's life seems too good to be true. As questions about her new relationship overwhelm her, so do thoughts of Atlas Corrigan—her first love.

The Midnight Library

By Matt Haig

Published: 2020

★★★★☆ (4.0/5)

Fantasy Fiction Contemporary

Between life and death there is a library, and within that library, the shelves go on forever. Every book provides a chance to try another life you could have lived. Nora Seed finds herself faced with this decision: What if she had made different choices? Would she have been happier?

The Alchemist

By Paulo Coelho

Published: 1988

★★★★★ (4.5/5)

Fantasy Fiction Classic

The Alchemist follows a young Andalusian shepherd named Santiago who dreams of finding a worldly treasure. His quest will lead him to riches far different—and far more satisfying—than he ever imagined. Santiago's journey teaches us about the essential wisdom of listening to our hearts and following our dreams.

Where the Crawdads Sing

By Delia Owens

Published: 2018

★★★★★ (4.8/5)

Mystery Fiction Literary Fiction

For years, rumors of the "Marsh Girl" have haunted Barkley Cove, a quiet town on the North Carolina coast. So in late 1969, when handsome Chase Andrews is found dead, the locals immediately suspect Kya Clark, the so-called Marsh Girl. But Kya is not what they say. Sensitive and intelligent, she has survived for years alone in the marsh.

The Four Agreements

By Don Miguel Ruiz

Published: 1997

★★★★★ (4.7/5)

Self-help Spirituality Personal Development

Based on ancient Toltec wisdom, The Four Agreements offer a powerful code of conduct that can rapidly transform our lives to a new experience of freedom, true happiness, and love. The Four Agreements are: Be Impeccable With Your Word, Don't Take Anything Personally, Don't Make Assumptions, Always Do Your Best.

The Subtle Art of Not Giving a Fuck

By Mark Manson

Published: 2016

★★★★☆ (4.0/5)

Self-help Nonfiction Psychology

In this generation-defining self-help guide, a superstar blogger cuts through the crap to show us how to stop trying to be "positive" all the time so that we can truly become better, happier people. The Subtle Art is a refreshing slap for a generation to help them lead contented, grounded lives.

The Hobbit

By J.R.R. Tolkien

Published: 1937

★★★★★ (4.7/5)

Fantasy Adventure Classic

Bilbo Baggins is a hobbit who enjoys a comfortable, unambitious life, rarely traveling any farther than his pantry or cellar. But his contentment is disturbed when the wizard Gandalf and a company of dwarves arrive on his doorstep to whisk him away on an adventure to reclaim the dwarves' mountain home from the dragon Smaug.
